2. About a City - Delhi

History

Delhi is one of India's most historically important cities. It has been the center of several empires and contains many famous historical monuments.

Culture

Delhi has a diverse culture influenced by people from many parts of India. Festivals, art and traditions make the city vibrant and unique.

Food

Delhi is famous for street food such as chaat, parathas, kebabs and many other delicious dishes.

Famous Places

India Gate, Red Fort, Qutub Minar and Lotus Temple are some of the most popular attractions.

Tourism

Delhi attracts visitors throughout the year because of its history, architecture, food and cultural diversity.
